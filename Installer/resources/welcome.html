Welcome to Stentor

Stentor restores voice recordings to clean, studio-quality speech using the open-source Sidon model, running entirely on your Mac's GPU — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

  • Speech is detected automatically and shown as green regions; only those are restored.
  • Everything else stays silent, so the output keeps the original length and lines up in your DAW.
  • A/B comparison, waveform and spectrogram views, looping and per-region re-rendering.

This installer copies Stentor.app into your Applications folder. On first launch Stentor downloads its voice model (about 930 MB, once).

Requirements: Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or later), macOS 14 Sonoma or newer, about 2 GB of free disk space.
