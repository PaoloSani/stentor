Stentor is installed

  1. Open Applications → Stentor.
  2. Drop a voice recording onto the window (or press ⌘O).
  3. Check the green regions, then press Enhance (⌘↩) and compare with B.
  4. Export with ⌘E.

On first launch Stentor downloads its voice model (about 930 MB) and prepares it for your GPU. This happens only once; updates keep the model.
